Simple Omelette Recipe

A quick and easy egg dish that’s perfect for breakfast, lunch, or dinner.

Preparation Time

  • Total: 10 minutes
  • Prep: 5 minutes
  • Cook: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2–3 large eggs
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tablespoon of butter or oil
  • Optional fillings: cheese, mushrooms, onions, peppers

Instructions

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl, add salt and pepper, and beat well.
  2. Heat butter or oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
  3. Pour in the eggs and tilt the pan to spread them evenly.
  4. Add your desired fillings while the eggs are still soft.
  5. Fold the omelette in half and cook for 1–2 more minutes.
  6. Slide onto a plate and serve warm.
