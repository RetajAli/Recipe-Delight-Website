Grilled Chicken Caesar Salad

A healthy and flavorful salad with grilled chicken, crispy romaine, Caesar dressing, and croutons.

Preparation Time

  • Total: 25 mins
  • Prep: 10 mins
  • Cook: 15 mins

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless chicken breasts
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 head romaine lettuce, chopped
  • 1/2 cup Caesar dressing
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 cup croutons
  • Lemon wedges (optional)

Instructions

  1. Season chicken with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Grill until cooked through and slightly charred.
  2. Let chicken rest for 5 minutes, then slice into strips.
  3. In a large bowl, combine romaine lettuce, Caesar dressing, and Parmesan cheese.
  4. Toss well to coat the lettuce evenly.
  5. Top with grilled chicken and croutons.
  6. Garnish with lemon wedges if desired. Serve immediately.
