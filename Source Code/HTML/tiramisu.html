Classic Tiramisu Recipe

A delicious Italian dessert with coffee-soaked ladyfingers and mascarpone cream.

Preparation Time

  • Total: 4 hrs 30 mins
  • Prep: 30 mins
  • Cook: None

Ingredients

  • 6 egg yolks
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 3/4 cup dark rum or Marsala
  • 1.5 cups mascarpone cheese
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup espresso
  • Ladyfinger cookies
  • Cocoa powder

Instructions

  1. Whisk yolks and sugar over steam, then add rum and let cool.
  2. Whip cream and mix with mascarpone and egg mix.
  3. Dip ladyfingers in coffee and layer with cream in dish.
  4. Repeat layers and chill for 4 hours.
  5. Dust with cocoa and serve.
