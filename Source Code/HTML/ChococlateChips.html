Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

Chewy, gooey, and full of melty chocolate chips—perfect with a glass of milk!

Preparation Time

  • Total: 45 mins
  • Prep: 15 mins
  • Cook: 10–12 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter (softened)
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 0.5 tsp salt
  • 2 cups chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Cream together butter and sugars until fluffy.
  3. Beat in eggs and vanilla extract.
  4. Mix flour, baking soda, and salt, then combine with wet ingredients.
  5. Fold in chocolate chips.
  6. Scoop dough onto baking sheet and bake for 10–12 mins.
  7. Let cool on a wire rack and enjoy!
