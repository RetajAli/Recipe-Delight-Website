Refreshing Drink Recipes

Lemon Mint Juice

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup fresh lemon juice
  • 1/2 cup fresh mint leaves
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 cups cold water
  • Ice cubes

Instructions:

  1. Blend lemon juice, mint, sugar, and water until smooth.
  2. Strain and pour into glasses over ice.
  3. Garnish with mint leaves and serve cold.

Strawberry Smoothie

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup fresh strawberries
  • 1 banana
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 tsp honey (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Place all ingredients into a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth and creamy.
  3. Pour into a glass and enjoy chilled.

Iced Coffee

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup brewed coffee (cooled)
  • 1/4 cup milk or cream
  • 1 tsp sugar or syrup
  • Ice cubes

Instructions:

  1. Fill a glass with ice.
  2. Pour in coffee, milk, and sugar. Stir well.
  3. Serve immediately.
