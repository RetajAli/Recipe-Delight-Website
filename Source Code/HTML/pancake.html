Fluffy Pancake Recipe

Light and fluffy pancakes that are perfect for breakfast with your favorite toppings.

Preparation Time

  • Total: 25 mins
  • Prep: 10 mins
  • Cook: 15 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp white sugar
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp melted butter

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, sift together the flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.
  2. Make a well in the center and pour in milk, egg, and melted butter. Mix until smooth.
  3. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat.
  4. Pour or scoop the batter onto the griddle, using approximately 1/4 cup for each pancake.
  5. Brown on both sides and serve hot with butter, syrup, or fruit.
